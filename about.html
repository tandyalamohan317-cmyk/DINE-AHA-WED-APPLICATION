Our Journey & Legacy

The Artistry of Luminous Epicure

Born from an uncompromising passion for culinary perfection, AHA is where time-honored Indian royal traditions meet futuristic glassmorphic architectural serenity.

The Genesis

Resurrecting Lost Imperial Recipes

Founded in 2024, AHA was conceptualized as an ode to Awadhi, Mughlai, and modern fusion gastronomy. We traveled across royal households and ancient spice routes to decode the precise alchemy behind slow-cooked Dum preparation.

In our kitchen, brass vessels hand-beaten by master artisans meet cutting-edge precision heat induction. No artificial tenderizers, no industrial bases — only 100% clarified mountain ghee, whole organic spices, and pure culinary patience.

"True luxury is patience in the pan and clarity on the plate."

— Master Chef Rohan Varma
